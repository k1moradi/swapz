## Safety and actual scheduling cooperation

1. Linux zswap owns **small configurable bounded compressed RAM cache** and exclusively controls real memory-pressure scheduling and eviction. It keeps useful compressed pages in RAM; on genuine eviction a previously stored dictionary-free LZ4 block **might later** be passed once to swapz backing. swapz is disk compression/packing and GC only; **no competing RAM reclaim/pressure controller, no new trigger/prefetch into swapz, no arbitrarily forced zswap evictions**.
2. Existing ordinary `RAW_4096` means the original UNCOMPRESSED 4096 bytes, never compressed LZ4. Future authenticated source compressed LZ4 uses RAW **lower staging/copy/I/O machinery** only under distinct *bounded volatile RAM* descriptor, stored codec/length/CRC32C/original STORE-time full 4096 SHA/allocator epoch/stored-entry identity/BIO lifetime/DM logical+generation+GC+ACK ownership. Unsupported codec ZSTD, dictionary mismatch, compressor change, too-large payload, invalid generation or CRC/SHA, late callbacks, swapoff/overwrite/reuse go to **existing original 4096 fallback** or propagate genuine lower error. Do not invent on-media format, claim a scalar bi_swap_origin identifies compressed bytes, use current ordinary RAW to store compressed stream, or falsely reconstruct original 4096 from compressed CRC only.
