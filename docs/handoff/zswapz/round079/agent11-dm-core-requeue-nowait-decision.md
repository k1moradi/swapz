## Decisive source facts and ownership

- [Original `dm_handle_requeue` lines 903–948](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/c339f4010dea8b0d754f84ace70cd1b089878ef8/drivers/md/dm.c#L903-L948), [original `__dm_io_complete` lines 950–998](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/c339f4010dea8b0d754f84ace70cd1b089878ef8/drivers/md/dm.c#L950-L998): `BLK_STS_DM_REQUEUE` is **not** `BLK_STS_AGAIN`.
