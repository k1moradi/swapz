## Design and safety frozen

Do not introduce competing shrinkers, pressure timers, forced upstream eviction, or zswap recompression. Future authentic original zswap volatile original LZO1X/raw LZ4 stream may be independently tagged `ORIGINAL_COMPRESSED_OPAQUE` and copied byte-for-byte before source erase, with protected independent decoder generation/lifetime; **do not call the opaque representation `RAW_4096`**, which ALWAYS denotes all 4096 original **uncompressed** page bytes. Do not equate original codec with swapz own delta/shuffle/LZ4 disk codec. Protect actual SWAPSPACE2 physical first4096 signature/UUID and partition table, exact 20-byte volatile map, lower payload-only extents.
