## Decision and source boundary

**GO for disabled candidate insertion and review of the original core patch; NO-GO for real selected-DM claim, live compressed routing, or physical admission.** The actual frozen broker [header](https://github.com/k1moradi/swapz/blob/cf5a539913fd169fd71bac72c61bc355285a51f6/src/kernel/zswap-original-ticket.h) explicitly prohibits calls under foreign locks. Its [claim implementation](https://github.com/k1moradi/swapz/blob/cf5a539913fd169fd71bac72c61bc355285a51f6/src/kernel/zswap-original-ticket.c#L282-L304) acquires its own trylock, compares key/current epoch/state and writes a non-atomic state under that lock. Calling it under queue_lock is prohibited; claiming before queue_lock would race admission closure, revoke and enqueue. A successful claim followed by failing or requeueing the ordinary clone is not a permissible fallback. No compatible atomic adapter or current core-owned carrier exists in the frozen tree.

## Minimal missing adapter and teardown/error audit

1. Core-managed retained candidate from the exact original BIO transaction, with explicit clone propagation and cancellation on split/partial/remap/reissue and every reject/free path. Current selected table/target epochs must be non-reused independently of pointer identity. Do not save an SRCU index or borrow the core's orig_bio past ->map.
2. An authorized nonblocking claim/revocation primitive usable **under queue_lock** that arbitrates the same ticket state with revocation, validates immutable source and current route/generation, and transfers an already-retained ref/credit into the initialized descriptor. Existing broker state transitions are lock-based, so do not bolt a CAS onto the current non-atomic enum. Generation is owned by the ordered authority path, not the lane-profile epoch; a compatible independently published generation fence is missing. Close/revoke must exclude unclaimed tickets without waiting for deferred original BIOs. No allocation, callbacks, waits, retry or broker lock under queue_lock. On claim success, perform the non-failing descriptor move and list_add_tail before unlocking; failed claim leaves ref with caller for release outside the lock. Do not claim until storage and terminal ownership are available. Never acquire claim then discover an unavailable queue/worker/credit.
3. Only an integrated claimed descriptor may bypass ordinary prep; route it to the ordered imported consumer before any recompression. On after-claim failure use the same real upper error completion, no ordinary recompression, second BIO or requeue. Existing swapz_complete retires prep before bio_endio and accounts upper result once; old per-BIO data cannot be used afterward. Existing ordered authority/lower FUA/flush publication must be preserved, with independent decoder/map/GC lifetime; successful lower callback alone is not durable ACK.
4. Existing swapz_drain closes accepting under queue_lock, cancels timer outside it, drains preparation/ordered/lower and completion, then checks incoming/credits/authority; dtr drains then frees context. Existing resume reopens only if not failed; lane-profile epoch is NOT a source route epoch. Core dm_submit_bio selects under SRCU, __split_and_process_bio picks ti and allocates clone, __map_bio retains native accounting, DM_MAPIO_KILL/REQUEUE frees clone; original suspend/table lifetime cannot be replaced by pointer equality. A future integration must revoke route before detach and drain claimed debt before destroying target. No actual imported debt exists in this candidate; no new drain wait is introduced.
