## zswap/swapz cooperation and safety boundary

A future *real* previously stored zswap LZ4 block may be transferred as the **actual compressed bytes** using already-safe swapz RAW **lower-I/O staging machinery only**, with separate bounded volatile RAM `ZSWAP_COMPRESSED_LZ4_V1` *conceptual* representation/codec/length/CRC32C/full original store SHA/allocator slot incarnation/BIO refcount/DM mapping generation+GC epoch, and matching LZ4 4096 original decoder. **Existing ordinary `RAW_4096` always means the FULL ORIGINAL UNCOMPRESSED 4096 bytes**, never variable compressed bytes. Initial reject, ordinary swap, stale or forged identity take existing uncompressed decompression→normal swapz path. Neither worker may auto-activate compressed transfer, change 4096 BIO byte count, make completion bypass, on-media headers, magic or metadata, or a second pressure policy.

Original **valid SWAPSPACE2 physical partition first 4096 bytes** (header, signature, UUID) and partition table immutable.
