## Architecture and frozen constraints

Original Linux MM/zswap alone owns compressed RAM residency and natural pressure eviction. swapz owns disk-only 4/8/16KiB payload packing and GC; no second shrinker, pressure controller, timers or recompression of authentic original compressed bytes. At pinned [mm/zswap.c:1005–1095](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/c339f4010dea8b0d754f84ace70cd1b089878ef8/mm/zswap.c#L1005-L1095), original zswap_writeback_entry allocates locked swapcache folio; checks same entry in XArray at1047–1051; decompresses at1053; erases at1058; frees at1064; marks folio uptodate; drops ref at1082; submits __swap_writeout/swap_write_submit at1085–1086. Therefore ordinary downstream swap BIO does not carry authenticated original compressed stream. Conventional RAW_4096 always complete ORIGINAL UNCOMPRESSED 4096. Original stored length4096 is original uncompressed page, not LZO compressed bytes. Original possible compressed lengths1..4095 (not claim every length occurs in real workload). Current SWAPZ_MAX_COMPRESSED=3568 rejects 3569..4095; never truncate or recompress original. Preserve correct original folio/writeback failure behavior: do not free last valid data representation before successor ownership guaranteed; proposed ordinary fallback must preserve normal MM path and cannot claim no recompression if conventional target chooses its normal codec. No forced RAW enable. Durable actual lower WRITE+FUA/flush, ordered publish, exactly-once actual upper completion, no fabricated receipts. Protect first physical SWAPSPACE2 4096 bytes/header/UUID/partition and exact 20-byte volatile ordinary map. No reboot persistence claim.
