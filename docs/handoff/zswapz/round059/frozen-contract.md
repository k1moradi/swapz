## Immutable architecture, correctness and safety

- Linux **native MM** owns normal zswap compressed-RAM retention/pressure eviction. Swapz owns only **disk** representation, packing, and GC; do not add a shrinker/forced swap-out/second reclaim policy or wait for an unrelated MM future event. Both codecs may differ; preserve source-selected original algorithm/driver, dictionary and framing/length. An already STORED compressed buffer may be reused **without recompression only after actual native MM STORED owner+BIO+current DM lease, decoder, lower durability, map, ACK and READ have independently proven lifetime**.
- Native conventional `RAW_4096` is **exactly 4096 ORIGINAL UNCOMPRESSED bytes** and always retains its old path. A future zswap STORED import must be a **separately tagged** RAM-only codec/version/profile identity and payload, never a disguised RAW or a serialized SWZ codec; check full original 4096 decoded SHA256, compressed CRC32C, source slot+generation/epoch, complete exact lower read bytes, no dictionary/trailing/unknown format and no partial or aliased output. Unsupported/no-auth before commitment must leave native normal full-page writeback intact. Post-commit corruption fails the upper read explicitly.
- Protect all physical backing bytes in the first **4096-byte SWAPSPACE2 header/signature/UUID** and the **parent partition table**; no raw device, repartition, mkswap, swapoff/on or loaded production target. No new persisted metadata. Keep normal 20-byte `swapz_map`, bounded volatile descriptors and current 4/8/16KiB physical payload+zero padding format.
- Own the original BIO and source bvec until no clone can reference them; a ticket/scalar is NOT tenure. The real target must own accepted `dm_per_bio_data`, current target-relative 8 sectors, queue/drain fences, lower endio and exactly one real upper BIO completion, not simulated callback. A selected LZO symbol is NOT proof its decoder/module survives zswap-pool teardown; original LZ4 and LZO1X both need explicit independent decoder-owner/read/GC pins. Real imported lower WRITE+FUA/flush, map generation/GC, actual upper ACK and independent full read are NOT yet qualified.
- Remain `SOURCE_MAPPED_AUTHENTICATED=0`, `PHASE_A=BLOCKED`, `DIRECT_COMPRESSED_HANDOFF=DISABLED`, `FORCED_RAW_BYPASS=DISABLED`.
