## Causality and lifetime

Reserve at the authoritative transition under its existing serialization where feasible; an append is bounded, synchronous and copies only precomputed metadata/digests. No alloc/hash/I/O, waiting, unrelated locks, raw pointer IDs or page/stream dumps in append. Reservation order is not completion order: unrelated parallel events are partially ordered by distinct operation/descriptor/clone/original/lower/pin identities and their real happens-before edges. Do not backdate, sort to repair, infer ACK, or use a failed reservation as evidence. If exact ordering or the actual event cannot be observed safely, mark capture incomplete and refuse positive qualification. A failed mint/append, any sticky loss, missing link or incomplete sink invalidates admission evidence; no effect on product progress. Disabled callers must check their separately scoped gate BEFORE constructing event/digest data. A disabled adapter call itself reads enabled once, does not touch counters or event input; not a claim of zero end-to-end overhead.

Caller-owned, disjoint preallocated slots; one owner serializes init/start/stop/drain/seal/export. Init leaves capture off; start once only. External lifetime must cover entrants BEFORE their first enabled load through return, including deferred worker and completion paths. Stop first; revoke all external publication; synchronous RCU/SRCU/worker and callback join, then seal. The callback is an owner-supplied obligation, not an internally inferred grace period. A failed join retains owner and storage; no seal/export/free. Release publication of the naturally aligned u32 slot marker follows the full record copy, acquired by seal. A paused writer does not prevent later slot publication. A missing marker refuses seal even after join. Export never substitutes partial slots, writes only fixed wire fields and includes sticky loss. It is a cold, owner-only operation, never a data-path callback.
